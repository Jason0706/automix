From the first note to a balanced mix

Automix sets every channel against all the others, the way finished records are balanced: the lead vocal first, then each instrument at its place below it.

  1. 0:00

    It listens

    It hears every channel and works out what's on it. Its guesses wait for you to confirm the names, once.

  2. 0:10

    The first mix

    Volume, panning, EQ and compression for every channel, from the first 10 seconds of playing.

  3. 0:30

    A better one

    With 30 seconds heard, it mixes again.

  4. Every 20–60 s

    It keeps up

    It re-checks the balance on everything heard so far and moves faders gently, only for a part that has really drifted.

  5. Between songs

    It fine-tunes

    In the pause, it refines EQ and compression and resets the main level for the next song.

Two modes

Pick one in the app's header: a short soundcheck, or none at all. Either way, Automix keeps the mix balanced through the whole set.

The engineer stays in charge

Automix sets every control, says why it changed each one, and gives way whenever you take over.

Every setting has three states, and you can switch any of them at any time.

A

Auto

Automix sets it and keeps it right.

T

Trim

Nudge it. Your change rides on top of Automix's setting.

L

Lock

Automix leaves it exactly where you put it.

Why · every change comes with a reasonFrom the mix above
LevelGuitar solo−0.7 dBIt carries the song while the singer rests, so it sits close under the vocal, well above the other guitars.
LevelHarmony vocalsabout −16 dB eachTwelve harmony parts share one level, since about nine sing at once.
EQLead vocal−4 dB at 1.1 kHzA resonance: it stood 9.1 dB above the sound around it.
CompBass4:1Evens it out: its loudest moments come down about 7 dB.
PanGuitarsL70 to R70Five electric guitar parts, spread apart so each has its own space.
MuteVox liveOffMarked unused (a scratch mic), so it stays out of the mix.

It never mutes on its own

A part that comes in late is mixed in after a few seconds of playing. Only a channel you mark unused is switched off.

It watches for feedback

On live inputs, a howl is caught as it starts: the main comes down and that frequency is notched out.

It has limits

Each channel has a ceiling Automix won't push past, and you can set your own. The main level aims for −16 LUFS and is re-set after every song, with a limiter on the peaks.
